## The short version

Two forms on this site collect an email address. Nothing else is collected,
there are no cookies, no tracking pixels and no advertising. Nobody's details
are sold or shared.

## The two forms do different things

**The enquiry form**, on the contact page, collects your name, your email
address and whatever you type in the message box. It is used to answer you,
and for nothing else.

**The register form**, the email band at the foot of most pages, collects only
your email address. It is used to tell you once, when the course is ready. If
you would rather not hear, do not use it, or ask to be removed at any point.

## Where your details actually go

Submissions are handled by Netlify, who host this site. They arrive as an
email notification and they are also stored in the site's account with
Netlify, where they stay until they are deleted. Netlify is a United States
company and the data is held on their servers, under their standard data
processing terms.

They are not passed to anybody else.

## How long they are kept

Enquiries are kept while they are being dealt with and for a reasonable period
afterwards, then deleted. Registrations are kept until the course launch
message has gone out, or until you ask to be removed, whichever comes first.

## Analytics

Visits are counted in a way that uses no cookies and identifies nobody. There
are no individual profiles, no cross-site tracking and no advertising
networks. That is why this site has no cookie banner: it does not need one.

## Getting your details removed

Email and ask. They will be deleted, and you do not have to give a reason.

## Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can ask what is held about you, ask for it to
be corrected or deleted, and complain to the Information Commissioner's Office
at ico.org.uk if you are not satisfied with the response.
