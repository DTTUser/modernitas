## What this site collects

If you fill in a form on this site, it collects your name, your email address and anything you type in the message box. Nothing else.

## What it is used for

Answering you. That is all. Your details are not sold, shared or added to a marketing list, and you will not be emailed about anything other than the thing you asked about.

## Who holds it

Dr Terry Critchley. Enquiries are delivered by email and are not stored in a database on this site.

## Analytics

This site counts visits in a way that does not use cookies and does not identify anybody. That is why there is no cookie banner.

## Getting your details removed

Email and ask. They will be deleted.
