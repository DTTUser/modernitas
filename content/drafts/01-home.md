## Headline

Understand what computers actually do before you decide what to do with them.

## Opening

Modern IT became modern by evolving, and it has not stopped. Jobs evolve with it. People put the working life of an IT skill at somewhere between three and five years, and AI is shortening that further.

That leaves two kinds of people in the same position. If you are coming into IT, you need a way in that is wider than one language or one platform. If you are already in it, you need to know how your corner connects to the rest.

This course is the whole landscape in ten modules: what the technology is, what it is good at, what it is bad at, and where it sits in a business. No coding, and no jargon for its own sake.

## Who it is for

- **Pre-IT apprenticeships.** A grounding in the whole field before, or alongside, an apprenticeship, so the specialism has something under it.
- **Career changers and returners.** Coming to IT from another field, or back after time away, and needing the grounding nobody ever gave you.
- **Teachers and employers.** Teaching the subject, or hiring into it, and wanting one coherent account of the field rather than ten disconnected ones.
