## Tab 1 — What it covers

Ten modules. Each one stands on its own; taken together they build the map of modern computing that most people are assumed to already have.

| Module | What it is about |
| --- | --- |
| Enterprise computing | Mainframes and high performance computing. Two thirds of the world's data passes through machines most courses never mention. |
| Data centres | Where the computing actually happens. |
| Cloud | What it is, and what it costs you. |
| Databases | How organisations hold what they know. |

## Tab 2 — Who it is for

Pre-IT apprentices, before or alongside the apprenticeship. Career changers and returners. Teachers and employers who want one coherent account of the field.

It is also a fair test for people already working in IT. If you can write a few sentences on most of the topics above, you have the breadth. If you cannot, that is the gap this closes.
