## Intro

Questions about the course, the books, or anything on the site.

## The form

Name, email address, and a message. Two fields and a box.

> **OPTION 15 — whether the form asks what the enquiry is about.**
> **A. Add a single dropdown: the course, the books, something else (recommended).** It costs the visitor one click and it tells Terry what is actually being asked before he opens the message.
> **B. No dropdown.**
> Why A: it is also the field that later routes enquiries to resellers without the form being rebuilt. Adding an option to a list is a minute's work; adding routing to a form that has no category is not.

## Where enquiries go

To Terry, at an address on the modernitas.co.uk domain, forwarded to itmit2025@gmail.com. His split differs depending on who finds the buyer, so nothing goes to OBS automatically.

> **Worth doing at launch.** Enquiries arriving at a domain address rather than the gmail one means he holds his own records, and the forwarding can be changed later in a minute. It also looks better on a card in front of resellers than a gmail address does.
