order: 5
---

This article outlines system availability and also discusses some sleight of hand used by people who claim super-fast recovery times after failures. These are more often than not *repair* times, but *full operational recovery* is often much longer. This fact is very important, given the cost per hour or minute a system is not fully functioning.

It also boils down to what MTTR (Mean Time to Recover/Repair/Restart etc.) really means.
