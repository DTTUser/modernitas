This topic has been the subject of more studies and surveys than the death of Elvis, with similar results, that is, none. In 2018, I devised a simple survey for high school females aged 14-18 with an extra not seen in others; free-form input.
The results and my conclusions can be read in the link below. Unfortunately, my name seems to have been expunged from this official CAS report but I can assure you I wrote the base questions of my own volition.

- [CAS: FEMALES AND COMPUTING/COMPUTER SCIENCE SURVEY (2018)](https://www.computingatschool.org.uk/media/lcsj15f2/femalesincomputingreport2018.pdf)

In short, the free form answers singled out ‘computing’ as ‘too geekish’, ‘boring’ and ‘requiring too much mathematics knowledge’, none of which apply to modern information technology when truthfully and accurately presented.
