This article introduces the increasingly important topic of *resilience* which means more than just keeping the IT show on the road with backups. In short, resilience is the ability to resume one’s business after a setback.

In boxing, resilience is the ability of a fighter to recover from a punch (IT normal recovery) or knock down (IT disaster recovery). However, it has connotations beyond just that, inasmuch as the boxer must prepare himself via tough training, a fight plan and coaching to avoid the knockdown and, should it happen, he should be fit enough to recover and rejoin the fray quickly enough to beat the 10 second count.

The article below examines the three main areas of IT resilience and how they coalesce as a 'trinity' to be handled as such. It is not a matter of which to have, it is a case of ‘*all or nothing’*. If you think that is expensive, try frequent downtime, with its attendant costs, instead.
